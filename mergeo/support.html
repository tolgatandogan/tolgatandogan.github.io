Frequently Asked Questions

Ads not loading?

Make sure you have an active internet connection and try again in a few minutes. Ad availability can occasionally vary by region.

I lost my game progress

Your progress is tied to this device. Reinstalling the app on the same device should restore your data automatically. Progress does not currently transfer between devices.

How do I get coins?

Earn coins by completing daily missions, opening the daily chest, leveling up your Season Pass, or watching short rewarded ads in the Shop.

How do I remove ads?

Visit the Shop and unlock permanent ad-free play using coins you've earned in-game — no real-money payment required.

The game crashes or freezes

Make sure you have the latest version of Mergeo installed. Try restarting the app or your device. If the issue persists, contact us with your device model and OS version.
