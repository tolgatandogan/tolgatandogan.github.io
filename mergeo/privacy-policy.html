Mergeo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

No Account Required

Mergeo does not require you to create an account or log in. The app generates an anonymous device identifier on first launch, which is used solely to save your progress, coins, and scores.

Data We Collect

We collect: an anonymous device identifier; gameplay data (scores, coin balance, mission and Season Pass progress); and basic diagnostic data (crash and error logs) to help us improve the app. We do not collect your name, email address, or any other personally identifiable information.

Advertising

Mergeo uses Google AdMob to show ads and reward you with in-game coins for watching them. AdMob may use device identifiers for ad delivery and, where applicable, ad personalization, in accordance with Google's own privacy policy.

Purchases

Mergeo does not process any real-money purchases. All in-app upgrades (such as the ad-free option) are unlocked using coins earned through gameplay or by watching ads — never with real currency.

Data Retention

Your game data is retained as long as you use the app. Server-side data associated with your anonymous device identifier may be retained for up to 12 months after your last activity.

Your Rights

You can request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at the email below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
